# Enterprise, Labs, and Organizations

The [Enterprise](https://oproxy.world/business) page advertises 7×18 support, a custom management entry, optimized APIs, dedicated quotas and routing, team cost governance, and data security/isolation.

The contact form requires organization, contact, email, and use case; phone/WeChat, expected volume, and notes are optional. An authorized representative should submit only necessary business information.

![Enterprise contact form](/images/oproxy-steps/business-contact-form.png)

*Figure: The public enterprise form. Submit only the business information needed for contact.*

Oproxy states that it does not use user content to train its own models and does not sell user data. Third-party model providers may process requests under their own policies. Organizations requiring no-training, dedicated upstreams, isolation, or confidentiality should arrange an enterprise channel and written terms before use.

Use separate keys, enforce IP/spending/rate/expiration limits, review usage and orders, minimize sensitive data, and disable affected keys before incident investigation.
