# Campus Ambassador Program Terms and Notice

**Updated: 2026-07-30**

This notice explains the eligibility, rewards, review requirements, and boundaries of the Campus Ambassador Program. By applying for or continuing to participate in the program, you acknowledge that you have read and understood the following terms.

## 1. Eligibility and application

- The program is available to users with supported school email accounts.
- Users must apply from the affiliate rebate page. Submitting an application does not guarantee approval.
- The platform may manually review account status, school email domain, usage history, and risk signals.
- Campus ambassador status is tied to the school email domain identified at approval. If the email becomes invalid, changes, or appears abnormal, the platform may review or suspend the status again.

## 2. Invite relationship and starting balance

- A campus ambassador code can still work as a regular affiliate code. Non-school-email users may register and bind the invite relationship with it.
- Only new invitees from the same school email domain as the ambassador can receive the 10 CNY starting balance.
- The campus starting balance is granted at most once per invitee and will not be duplicated by rebinding, reapplying, or refreshing status.
- If the invite relationship, email domain, or account status does not meet the requirements, the campus starting balance will not be granted.

## 3. Campus cashback

- When a same-school invitee completes a valid recharge order, the campus ambassador earns cashback equal to 12% of that recharge amount.
- Campus cashback is credited as affiliate rebate quota and can be transferred to account balance according to the rules shown on the affiliate rebate page.
- Campus cashback does not stack with ordinary affiliate rebate. A qualifying recharge is calculated under the campus ambassador rule first.
- Redeem codes, manual admin adjustments, affiliate quota transfers, API usage deductions, and balance changes that are not recharge orders are not included in the 12% campus cashback base.
- For refunded, reversed, abnormal, or risky orders, the platform may withhold, delay, freeze, or recover related rewards.

## 4. Levels and identity changes

- The program does not include multi-level cashback. If A invites B and B invites C, A earns only from B’s qualifying recharge, and B earns only from C’s qualifying recharge.
- Cashback that B earns from C is not treated as B’s consumption, recharge, or order amount, and will not generate further cashback for A.
- If an invitee later becomes a campus ambassador, the existing invite relationship remains unchanged. Whether the original inviter earns campus cashback still depends on same-school status, valid recharge orders, and other requirements.

## 5. Compliance requirements

- Campus ambassadors should recommend the service truthfully, voluntarily, and lawfully, without false promises, misleading pricing, exaggerated claims, or impersonating the platform.
- Self-invites, bulk registrations, buying, selling, or borrowing school emails, fake school identities, induced refunds, fake orders, arbitrage, and spam promotion are prohibited.
- If abnormal behavior is detected, the platform may reject the application, revoke ambassador status, freeze or recover rewards, and handle related accounts under the platform terms.

## 6. Program nature and changes

- Campus ambassadors are not employees, agents, or authorized representatives of the platform, and may not make commitments on behalf of the platform.
- Reward amounts, rates, eligibility scope, review standards, and payout timing may be adjusted based on operations, risk control, or compliance needs.
- The platform will try to communicate material changes through pages, announcements, or in-app notices. Actual execution is subject to system records and final platform review.

## 7. Data use and support

- To determine eligibility and calculate rewards, the platform processes necessary account information, school email domains, invite relationships, recharge orders, and rebate records.
- The data is used only for account service, program review, reward issuance, risk control, and support verification where necessary.
- For questions about applications, review results, or reward records, please contact support through the platform support channel.
