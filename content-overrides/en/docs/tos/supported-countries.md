# Registration Eligibility, Schools, and Support

Oproxy currently accepts users with a valid invitation and required verification, supported education emails, or approved enterprises, labs, and research teams. The service is not offered to users under 18.

The [Support](https://oproxy.world/support) page provides school/organization search plus country, province, and city filters and lists recognized email domains. The list changes, so it is not duplicated here.

If the school or domain is missing, contact support for manual review. Provide the school name and domain, but never a password or verification code. Organizations should use the Enterprise form.

- Email: `support@oproxy.world` (the support page states a response within 96 hours)
- WeCom: link on the support and enterprise pages
- Tickets: available after sign-in

Core model calls require cross-border processing. Confirm that your location, organization policy, and data type permit it before use.
